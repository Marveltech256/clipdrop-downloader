YouTube downloads, explained

How do I download a YouTube video?

Paste a YouTube video or Shorts URL, choose Fetch video, and select an available format. Once processing finishes, choose Save video. Only download content you own or have permission to save.

Which formats and qualities are available?

ClipDrop lists the MP4, WebM, and audio-only streams available for each video. Quality may range from low resolution to HD or 4K when the source offers it. Separate video and audio tracks are merged automatically; not every video offers every format.

Why can a video be unavailable?

YouTube may restrict automated requests or require sign-in. Private, age-restricted, region-blocked, and removed videos may be unavailable. Refresh the link and try again later if a temporary error occurs.

Does ClipDrop work on phones?

The page adapts to mobile and desktop browsers. File saving depends on your browser and device. Higher-quality videos use more data and take longer to prepare.

Is ClipDrop affiliated with YouTube?

No. ClipDrop is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
