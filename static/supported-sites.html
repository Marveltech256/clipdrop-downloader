Sources · Extension

Supported video sources

ClipDrop uses yt-dlp to extract available public video and audio streams. Support changes when source websites change. A listed extractor does not guarantee every video will download.

Public video pages

  • YouTube: individual videos and Shorts. Hosting-provider traffic may receive a verification challenge, even for a public video.
  • Vimeo: public video pages when the source makes a compatible stream accessible. Private and password-protected videos are not supported by this app.
  • Dailymotion: public video pages where a downloadable stream is available. Geographic and account restrictions can prevent extraction.
  • Other public websites: paste a single video page. yt-dlp may recognize its extractor or embedded player; unsupported pages return an error.

Direct video links

A link ending in a video extension such as .mp4 or .webm can work when it returns a public media file. Signed links can expire. The Chrome extension can also find file URLs exposed by the current page and send them directly to Chrome’s download manager.

Formats and quality

The source determines available quality. ClipDrop lists stream containers such as MP4 and WebM, along with audio-only formats when present. Separate tracks are merged when a compatible audio stream exists. Container names do not guarantee a particular codec, resolution or device compatibility.

What is not supported?

DRM-protected media, paywall bypasses, private-network URLs, private accounts, subscription access and ongoing live streams are not supported. Playlists and profiles must be replaced with a single-video URL.

Only save material you own or are permitted to download, and respect the source’s terms.
